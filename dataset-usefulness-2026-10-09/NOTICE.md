# Scope and attribution

The MIT license covers the original numerical starters, tests, diagram scripts, diagrams and explanatory documentation authored for this packet. It does not relicense any referenced dataset, checkpoint, upstream source, competition material or other author's notebook/card.

Data references: NCBI PubChem; EMBL-EBI ChEBI; LIPID MAPS; and the existing public `prvsiyan` datasets named in the cards. Their own terms and citation requirements apply. No data arrays, structure tables, spectra or model weights are included here.

Documentation examples inspected: Dmitrii Gluzdov's CASMI popularity prior, natural-product panel and fold-safe FPNet; Samar Talwar's harmonized MassBank; and verracodeguacas' Gemma budget-fit notebook. The packet uses original wording and original starter code, rather than reproducing those descriptions or notebooks. Exact source links are supplied beside the observations.

The large public Kaggle descriptions saved outside this publication allowlist are internal research reference material. Do not publish them as part of this original packet.

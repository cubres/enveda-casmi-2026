# Data and parameter scope

This release includes source, scalar results and two small completed reciprocal-
rank arrays. It does not bundle spectra, molecular records, fingerprints, model
weights or optimizer state. The validation arrays are derived outputs of the
linked public training/development inputs; they are outside the source-code
Apache grant. Use them with the applicable input terms and preserve this test's
provenance and uncertainty.

The public full-bank and split cards scope their data permissions by component;
they do not make all upstream records Apache-2.0. Keep the bank's competition and
COCONUT provenance. The selected V6 and V9 parameter cards use explicit component
permissions under Kaggle's Other selection; do not infer a parameter license
from the source-code footer. Retrieve the original cards with the pinned public
versions in `REPRODUCE.md`. This packet grants no new rights in upstream data or
third-party code and does not replace those cards.

The exact completed output hashes in `PUBLIC_RESULT.json` distinguish measured
artifacts from future forks. No leaderboard score, native-serving eligibility,
pure training effect or statistically established gain is claimed.

# Attribution and release provenance

The CASMI preparation, training, ranking and serving implementations in the accompanying source release are maintained by **prvsiyan / cubres**, subject to the file-specific notices and provenance manifest. This attribution does not imply ownership of third-party input data or endorsement by its providers.

- **Enveda CASMI 2026 organizers and source-library contributors:** [competition and data access](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/enveda-CASMI26-molecule-id-mass-spectra). Cite the competition and preserve individual library attribution where supplied. Training inputs and hidden evaluation data have distinct permitted uses.
- **ChEBI, EMBL-EBI:** Malik et al. (2025), *ChEBI: re-engineered for a sustainable future*, and the [publisher's citation/licensing guidance](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi/about/). Current database terms: CC BY 4.0. Record the actual release URL, retrieval date, SHA256 and CHEBI identifiers for a rebuilt bank.
- **LIPID MAPS:** Conroy et al. (2023), *LIPID MAPS: update to databases and tools for the lipidomics community*, DOI [10.1093/nar/gkad896](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkad896), plus [resource-specific citations](https://www.lipidmaps.org/how-to-link). Current database terms: CC BY 4.0. Preserve LM identifiers and describe filtering, standardization, deduplication and fingerprint generation.
- **COCONUT / Steinbeck group and contributors:** [COCONUT](https://coconut.naturalproducts.net/about). Current curated-data terms: CC0; infrastructure code: MIT. Record the downloaded release and its checksum separately from derived Kaggle arrays and bit-selection files.
- **RDKit contributors:** [RDKit](https://www.rdkit.org/). Preserve the software's license and pin the exact version used to generate descriptors. Matching matrix dimensions alone cannot establish matching fingerprints.

The original 2026-09 candidate-bank source snapshots are not established merely by consulting today's provider pages. The cards distinguish retained source recipes, newly reconstructed export bridges, existing checkpoint bytes and unverified historical source manifests. No missing source date, split, hash or accuracy result should be inferred from a filename.

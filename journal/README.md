# Journal: Enveda CASMI 2026

This folder holds sanitized campaign documents for the Enveda CASMI 2026 competition (molecule identification from mass spectra, notebooks only). It is a snapshot as of 2026-10-10. Official-row data comes from the campaign ledger up to its last dated line, 2026-10-10 11:27:29 UTC. The coordinator for this campaign was an AI coding agent working under the owner's direction.

Private identifiers (worker names, account identifiers, internal session and run identifiers, local paths) are removed. Row ids, scores, dates and UTC times, and public notebook slugs are kept. Third-party text is paraphrased.

## Files

- `2026-10-08-frontier-sweep.md`: the public frontier on the day of the sweep, the verification of the sweep's levers, and the claims that were refuted.
- `2026-10-09-strategy.md`: the strategy synthesis: levers kept, refuted and repaired, the execution order, and the probabilities as the sources state them.
- `2026-10-10-state.md`: every Enveda official row to date with its role and raw status, the F1 redraw series and its decision, what is running and what is next.
- `lessons.md`: engineering lessons, each with the situation, the rule adopted, the evidence and how it was checked.

## Label legend

- [V] checked against source, metadata, a ledger row or a computed output
- [P] another author's claim (forum post, other notebook, single submission)
- [I] the author's inference

The frontier notes also use two tags, kept as written: [R] (another author's own single submission, the same status as [P]) and [O] (one of our official rows).

Scores are displayed public-leaderboard scores on about 132 molecules, so one molecule at rank 1 is worth about 0.0076.
